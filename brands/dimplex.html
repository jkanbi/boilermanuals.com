Dimplex boiler manuals on this page span the Electricaire range. Combi boilers are listed, and about half of them open a checked PDF. Each row carries its GC number (most Dimplex codes here begin 41-149 or 47-149), so the GC number on the data badge finds the matching Dimplex boiler manual.
