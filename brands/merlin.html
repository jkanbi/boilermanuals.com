Merlin Boiler Manuals

Our Merlin boiler manuals cover the floor standing 45/65, 65/95 and 100/150 range, for oil models. 6 of the 16 Merlin models link to a Merlin boiler manual we have opened and checked against the model; we are still looking for the Merlin 2000 and Internal Wall Hung 40/65 manuals, which have a Request manual link. No GC numbers are listed for these models, so a Merlin boiler manual request should quote the model name and PCDB number instead. The PCDB entries date from 1994 and include models still in production.
