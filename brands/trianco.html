Trianco Boiler Manuals

Trianco boiler manuals on this page span the Eurostar, TRO, Contractor and Iona ranges (oil, natural gas, electric, wood pellets and LPG). Combi, system, regular (heat-only) and external boilers are listed, and more than half of them open a checked PDF. Each row carries its GC number (most Trianco codes here begin 41-898 or 37-898), so the GC number on the data badge finds the matching Trianco boiler manual.

Got a combi boiler? Cut your gas bill in 2 minutes

Rows marked CCB are condensing combis. Combis waste gas when the flow temperature is set too high. MyBoiler’s free Boiler Optimiser shows the setting to try and how much you could save.
