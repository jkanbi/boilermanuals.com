Turkington Boiler Manuals

Our Turkington boiler manuals cover the Turco and Eurocal Ambassador, Countryman, Consul and Senator condensing ranges (oil). 13 of the 71 Turkington models link to a Turkington boiler manual we have opened and checked against the model; we are still looking for the Engineering Eurocal, Turco 2000 and Trojan manuals, which have a Request manual link. No GC numbers are listed for these models, so a Turkington boiler manual request should quote the model name and PCDB number instead. Models on the page include the Engineering and Turco ranges. The PCDB entries date from 1996 and include models still in production.
