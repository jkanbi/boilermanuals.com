A boiler counts when its GC number or PCDB index is on a row that links a manual we host; brochures and spec sheets don’t count. Solid fuel and biomass boilers, range cooker boilers and micro-CHP units are counted by PCDB index. The PCDB total is all 7,455 boiler records in PCDB tables 105, 122, 131 and 143. Electric boilers are not in the PCDB, so they are shown against the models we list and are not part of the PCDB figure. Heating controls are counted separately and are not included in the boiler totals. Data: SAP 10 PCDB revision 599, updated 30 September 2026.
