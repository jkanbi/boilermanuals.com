About

This microsite was created to provide literature resources for boilers. Here you may find installation & servicing manuals, user manuals and other technical documents. On this site, you will also find manuals for heating controls that are commonly used with boilers and also complete heating systems ie Drayton, Honeywell etc

This site is an extension of MyBoiler.com, where multiple resources are provided in connection with your home boiler and heating systems. Please visit MyBoiler.com for the full range of resources and services that we provide.

A boiler counts when its GC number or PCDB index is on a row that links a manual we host; brochures and spec sheets don’t count. Electric boilers are not in the PCDB, so they are shown against the models we list and are not part of the PCDB figure. Heating controls are counted separately and are not included in the boiler totals. Data: SAP 10 PCDB revision 599, updated 30 September 2026.
