About BoilerManuals.com

This microsite was created to provide literature resources for boilers. Here you may find installation & servicing manuals, user manuals and other technical documents. On this site, you will also find manuals for heating controls that are commonly used with boilers and also complete heating systems ie Drayton, Honeywell etc

This site is an extension of MyBoiler.com, where multiple resources are provided in connection with your home boiler and heating systems. Please visit MyBoiler.com for the full range of resources and services that we provide.

Who runs BoilerManuals.com

BoilerManuals.com is run by the team behind MyBoiler.com, a UK home heating site. It is a free reference library, with no registration and no paywall. Questions and manual requests go to manuals@myboiler.com.

How we check manuals

  • Every boiler we list: gas and oil boilers come from the UK Product Characteristics Database (PCDB, SAP 10), so the model names, fuels and GC numbers match the official register. Electric boilers and heating controls are not in the PCDB, so they are listed and counted separately.
  • A manual is linked only after the PDF is checked: we open it and confirm it names the same model or GC number and the same fuel as the row. Anything we can’t confirm stays unlinked and shows Request manual.
  • Natural gas and LPG: one manual is linked to both fuels only when the PDF lists both, or shows the LPG GC number.
  • Brochures are not manuals: brochures, spec sheets and price lists are never counted as manuals.
  • Hosted by us: every manual is served from our own server (cdn.boilermanuals.com), so links don’t break when a manufacturer changes its website. Each file is stored once and checked for duplicates before upload.
  • Corrections: every row has a Report link. If a manual looks wrong, tell us and we’ll recheck it.

Manuals are provided for reference. In the UK, work on a gas boiler must be carried out by an engineer on the Gas Safe Register.
